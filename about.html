About Leshky Records

Sound to Legacy.

Our Story

Leshky Records is a Nigerian record label based in Makurdi, Benue State. We are focused on discovering talented artists, developing their careers and creating music with a lasting impact.

Our goal is to build a home for exceptional African talent while connecting artists and audiences through great music, creativity and culture.

Our Mission

To discover, develop and promote exceptional musical talent.

Our Vision

To build a respected African music brand that turns sound into legacy.

Our Location

Makurdi, Benue State, Nigeria 🇳🇬

Our Values

Creativity, excellence, integrity, culture and artist development.
