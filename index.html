About Leshky Records

FROM SOUND
TO LEGACY.

Leshky Records is a Nigerian record label based in Makurdi, focused on discovering, developing and promoting talented artists.

Our mission is to create music that connects with people while giving artists the platform, development and opportunities they need to build sustainable careers.

OUR VISION

To build a respected African music brand that discovers exceptional talent and turns creative sound into a lasting legacy.


OUR HOME

Makurdi, Benue State, Nigeria 🇳🇬
